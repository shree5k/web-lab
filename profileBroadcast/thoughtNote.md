my subscription is shared across friends, family, cousins, it’s practically a public library card at this point.

and the other day, while reading about instagram’s new notes feature, and thought,

what if we had something like that here? a way to share what you’re watching, loving, or rewatching for the tenth time. not a full-blown post or a curated list, but just… a quiet broadcast.

a small, low-pressure way to share what you’re watching.
something lightweight, almost effortless, yet deeply human.

a simple way to express your favourites, your hidden gems, your guilty pleasures – to anyone who happens to peek into your corner of the app that day.

this may not work for many reasons, interaction wise, and most people might not even have that many profiles. it’s just a fun exploration.
