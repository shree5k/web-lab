(part 1)
sometimes the way i find something to watch isn’t by title or cast, it’s just the genre i’m in the mood for. i’ll open the app or even google best thriller movies on ott right now. i jump from one genre to another, hoping something sticks.

but genre always sits there like a secondary tag, useful but not really the hero. what if it wasn’t hidden in the details? what if genre itself was the doorway, the thing you interact with first, not last?

so, imagine a venn diagram with one circle for what it is, another for what i’m feeling. crime + cozy. sci-fi + slow burn. animation + heartbreak. mix two, get a suggestion born from the overlap.

(part 2)
back in the CD Disc days, we literally handed each other mixes. but movies never got that treatment. music became playlists; movies stayed one-off recommendations. cinephiles on letterboxd do this already with lists and curation. but imagine if mixes were actual objects again, tiny digital discs you could pass around, swap, collect. vibes, moods, themes, actors, genres, all turned into little discs you could bundle and share.

what if movies got their version of the playlist? what if sharing movies felt more like sharing a CD again, maybe CD mixes become pages (convergence). i’m just poking at the idea.

and for the implementation nerds: the venn discovery runs on simple geometry. we track each genre chip’s vertical distance from center (dy), then solve for its horizontal stretch using the circle equation x² + y² = r². the visual radius is larger than the math radius, creating a hyper-curve that keeps elements from clipping while still feeling satisfyingly circular.
